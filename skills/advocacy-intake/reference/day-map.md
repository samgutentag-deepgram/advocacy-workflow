# The day map

Nominal. Per-campaign reality goes in the campaign file.

| Day | Phase | What happens | Why then |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **0** | hot | Project done. Debrief. Capture manifest written. | The build is fresh and the memory of it evaporates within a day. |
| **1** | hot | First drop: the shortest-shelf-life, highest-heat piece. The personal narrative and the community post. | Launch attention is the one thing that does not keep. |
| **10** | hot ends | Second drop: the technical piece and the video. | These take longer to make and do not decay, so they do not need day 1. |
| **30** | cool | Third drop: derived surfaces, clips, the live demo. Often the second cycle's output. | The material exists; this is distribution, not creation. |
| **60** | cool | Fourth drop: the next angle on the same project. | This is the long tail. Skipping it is how one launch becomes one blog post and silence. |
| **90** | cool | Read the metric against the baseline. Retro. | The funnel needs time to show. Reading it earlier reads noise. |

Two things this map is deliberately not:

- **Not a publishing calendar.** Days are earliest-eligible, not deadlines. A drop that is not ready
  waits, and the campaign does not stall behind it.
- **Not a requirement that every campaign fills every slot.** A small project earns three drops and
  no day 60.

## Gate to drop day

The board groups by gate, not by surface, so drop days belong to gates.

| Gate | Drop day | Filed by | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A, the blogs | 1 | intake | The day map's day 1 drop is "the personal narrative". Both blogs are the source everything else projects from, so they go first. |
| B, the canonicals | 10 | intake | The day map's day 10 drop is "the technical piece and the video". A canonical is derived work and cannot start before its blog is approved. |
| C, the variants | 30 | cycle | The day map's day 30 is "derived surfaces, clips" and calls it distribution rather than creation. That is exactly what a style variant is. |
| D, the renders | 45 | cycle | Between the day 30 derived surfaces and the day 60 long tail. Renders wait on the video script variants, which are Gate C. |

Gates A and B are filed at promotion, when the two blogs and five canonicals
are already knowable. Gates C and D are filed by `advocacy-cycle` on its first
run, because a style variant of a canonical that does not exist yet is a
guess, not a task.
