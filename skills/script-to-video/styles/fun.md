# Fun and inviting

## Stance
Somebody made a thing they like and wants to show you. Warm, unhurried, faintly amused.

## Leads with
The friction. The annoyance that started it, stated plainly, before any mechanism.

## Emphasize
- Delight. Name the favourite file, the best moment, the part that still works on you.
- Everyday detail that places the thing in a real life. A commute, a school run, a coffee.
- Self-aware asides, one per beat at most.

## Cut
Jargon that has not been introduced. Numbers that need a figure to land.

## Register
First person, contractions throughout, sentences that could be said out loud without a breath.
This is the only style where an aside in parentheses earns its place.

## Length
Slightly longer than technical. Warmth costs words.

## Calibration line
> This one is my favourite file in the project.
